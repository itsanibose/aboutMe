A little about my work

For eighteen years I have worked at the seam between enterprise systems of record and modern cloud data platforms — the place where SAP business processes meet Databricks, and where raw operational data becomes something leaders can actually decide on.

I design the architecture, lead the teams that build it, and stay close enough to the engineering to know whether what we drew on the whiteboard will survive contact with production. Senior leaders at IBM, British Petroleum, Lexmark, Medtronic, Cencora, and Becton Dickinson have trusted me to set data strategy and carry high-stakes initiatives from first sketch to sustained adoption.

The through-line across all of it: depth in both data architecture and the ERP business processes — SD, MM, FI and GL — so the platform reflects not just clean data, but the business logic that makes the data mean something.
